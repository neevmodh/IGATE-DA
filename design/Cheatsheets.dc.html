Cheat Sheets

Machine Learning

Bias-variance: high bias=underfit, high variance=overfit. Precision=TP/(TP+FP), Recall=TP/(TP+FN), F1=2PR/(P+R). PCA: eigenvectors of covariance matrix, ordered by variance explained.

Probability and Statistics

Bayes: P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B). Binomial mean=np, var=np(1-p). Normal: 68-95-99.7 rule.

Linear Algebra

Eigenvalues: det(A−λI)=0. Rank = number of independent rows/cols. SVD: A=UΣVᵀ.
